# Character Design Reference: The Boy (Leo)

This document serves as the master reference guide for generating consistent images of the main character for the storybook.

## 1. Core Physical Attributes (Age ~4-5)

*   **Skin Tone:** Light brown/tan, warm undertones.
*   **Hair Texture & Style:** Dark brown, tightly coiled/curly hair. The cut is short/faded on the sides and back, with longer curls on the top. 
*   **Eyes:** Big, expressive, dark brown eyes.
*   **Distinctive Visual Anchor:** A slight gap between his front teeth when smiling, and one small curl that falls slightly forward on his forehead.

## 2. Canonical Outfit
*   **Default Outfit:** A red t-shirt with a white '13' printed on the front, classic blue jeans, and white sneakers with small red stripes.

## 3. Proportion Notes
*   **Age 4-5 Proportions:** Head is large, about one-quarter of total body height; short arms and legs; small hands; rounded shoulders.

## 4. Expression Range
*   **Default/Smiling:** Wide, joyful, and enthusiastic smile showing his slight tooth gap.
*   **Other Expressions:** Even when surprised, worried, or thoughtful, his face remains soft and open, with large expressive eyes and rounded brows.

## 5. Art Style Guidelines (Comic-Style)

When generating new illustrations of this character, always append the following style rules to ensure visual consistency:

> "Comic-book style illustration. Thick bold black ink outlines on clothing and objects, thinner softer outlines on the face and skin. Flat cel-shading with vibrant but slightly muted colors. Soft blended gradients and airbrush-like transitions on the face and skin with subtle rosy cheeks. Halftone dot textures and light cross-hatching for depth (sparingly on the face). Hand-drawn textured paper feel. High detail, clean lines, no photorealism."

## 6. Consistency / Negative Prompt

To prevent the character from drifting between generations, explicitly state what should NOT happen:
> "Do not make him photorealistic, older than preschool age, pale-skinned, straight-haired, overly muscular, anime-styled, or with sharp angular facial features. Avoid changing his eye color, hair texture, or core face shape."

## 7. Reference Sheet Instruction

Before using him in scenes, generate a clean character sheet using this prompt structure:
> "Create a character reference sheet showing front view, side view, three-quarter view, full-body pose, close-up face, and three expressions: joyful, curious, and surprised of a 4-year-old boy with light brown skin, short curly dark brown hair, and big brown eyes. Comic-book style illustration..."

## 8. How to Update / Age Up the Character

If you need to generate images of the character as he gets older (e.g., Age 8-10):
*   **Proportions:** Reduce the head-to-body ratio to about one-sixth of total height. Make the limbs longer and leaner.
*   **Facial Structure:** Elongate the face slightly, reducing the roundness (baby fat) in the cheeks. Lessen the prominence of the "rosy cheeks."
